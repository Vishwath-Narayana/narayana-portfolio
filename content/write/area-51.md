Visiting Area 51 with my friends is on my bucket list.

You cannot actually go in. It is a restricted military site in the Nevada desert, and the signs around it are serious. The trip is about the road to it: the long, empty highway that has been renamed the Extraterrestrial Highway, the tiny town of Rachel, and the bar there that has leaned into the whole legend.

I know most of it is myth and jokes. That is part of the fun. It is the one place on the map that people argue about without ever having seen it, and I would like to stand at the edge of it with people I like and laugh about it.

It also fits with the sky. Nevada is dark and empty, and the stars out there should be spectacular. A trip that mixes a joke, a road and a night of astrophotography sounds about right to me.

No date yet. First I need to find the friends who are willing to drive that far for a photograph of a fence.
