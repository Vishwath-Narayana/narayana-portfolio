---
title: Why Switzerland
date: 2026-10-02
tag: Life
summary: The country I want to settle in for good, and the reasons I keep coming back to it.
---
I want to live in Switzerland, and not for a year or two. I want to settle there for the rest of my life.

Part of it is the landscape. Mountains, lakes and long valleys, and light that changes by the hour. For someone who photographs ordinary light, that is hard to beat.

Part of it is how the place runs. The trains arrive when they say they will, and the network reaches villages most countries would give up on. I photograph railway lines for fun, so I have a soft spot for a country that treats them as a point of pride.

Part of it is the work. Zurich and Geneva have strong engineering and data communities, and I want to build a career in data engineering in a place that takes quality seriously.

None of this is simple. Moving countries means visas, language, rent and a lot of patience, and I know that. This is the long plan, the one the other plans bend around.

For now it sits at the back of everything I do: learn well, build things that last, and keep the photographs coming. I will write about the steps as I take them.
